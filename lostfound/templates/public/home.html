  1. {% icon "file" "icon-lg" %}

Good to know

Reporting guidance

{% icon "package" %} Found something?

Report it, then hand it to the campus security office. Please don't arrange handovers directly with people from the board.

{% icon "lock" %} Keep proof to yourself

Leave serial codes, engravings, stickers and contents out of public descriptions. They're how the real owner proves it's theirs.

{% icon "hourglass" %} Unclaimed items

Items are held for a set retention period. After that the team reviews them and an administrator authorises disposal or donation.
