How these figures are calculated

Return rate is returned found reports divided by all found reports, excluding reports the finder withdrew. Time to return runs from the found report's creation to handover completion. Verification failure rate is failed attempts over all attempts; a high rate can signal false claims. Hotspots count lost reports by location and by the hour they were lost.
